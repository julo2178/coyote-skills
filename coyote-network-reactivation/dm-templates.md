# DM Templates — Network Reactivation

5 archetypes × 2 languages, with reply-handling guidance per archetype.

**Length target**: 60-120 words per DM. Longer reads as ask, shorter reads as bulk.

**Variables to fill**:
- `[First name]` — their first name
- `[Your name]` — your first name
- `[specific moment]` — concrete shared memory, never generic
- `[current company]` — their current employer
- `[year-ish]` — approximate year of original relationship

---

## Quick decision tree

| Their role vs yours back then | Use template |
| --- | --- |
| They managed you | #1 — Ex-manager |
| Same level, worked closely | #2 — Ex-peer |
| You managed them | #3 — Ex-direct-report |
| They were a client | #4 — Ex-client |
| Cross-functional, faded over time | #5 — Reintroduction |

**Three pre-send checks:**
1. Does the opener reference a specific shared moment, not a generic memory?
2. Is the close a low-pressure question, not a meeting ask?
3. Is the mission / product / ask left out entirely?

---

## #1 — Ex-manager (they were senior to you)

**Tone**: respectful, references something they taught or led, no ego, low ask.

### EN

```
Hi [First name],

It's been a while. Your name came up the other day when I was thinking about [specific moment, e.g. "the way you ran the [project] launch in 2019" / "how you handled the [decision] call"]. That stayed with me, and I realised I never properly thanked you for it.

I saw you're now at [current company]. Congrats on the move. Would love to hear how it's going and what's on your mind these days.

No agenda, just keeping in touch with people who shaped how I work.

Best,
[Your name]
```

### FR

```
Salut [First name],

Ça fait un moment. Ton nom m'est revenu récemment en repensant à [moment précis, ex. « la façon dont tu as cadré le lancement de [projet] en 2019 » / « ta gestion de l'arbitrage sur [sujet] »]. C'est resté, et je me suis dit que je ne t'avais jamais vraiment remercié pour ça.

J'ai vu que tu es maintenant chez [current company]. Bravo pour la suite. Curieux d'avoir de tes nouvelles et de savoir ce qui t'occupe en ce moment.

Pas d'agenda, juste l'envie de garder le lien avec les gens qui ont compté.

À très vite,
[Your name]
```

**Reply handling**: ask one question about *their* current work, then share a one-liner about yours (mentioning your current activity is OK here, but factually, not as pitch). Never lead with what you want.

## #2 — Ex-peer (same level, worked closely)

**Tone**: warm, equal, can be slightly casual. Shared trench memory works well.

### EN

```
Hey [First name],

Long time. I was [context that triggered the thought, e.g. "reviewing some old slides from the [project] days" / "chatting with someone about [domain]"] and you crossed my mind, specifically [specific shared moment, e.g. "that nightmare deploy weekend" / "the day we rewrote the whole spec in 4 hours"].

Noticed you're at [current company] now. How's that going? What are you into these days?

Would be good to catch up properly at some point.

[Your name]
```

### FR

```
Salut [First name],

Ça fait longtemps. Je [contexte, ex. « relisais des vieilles specs de l'époque [projet] » / « discutais avec quelqu'un de [domaine] »] et j'ai repensé à toi, surtout à [moment précis, ex. « ce week-end de déploiement de l'enfer » / « le jour où on a réécrit toute la spec en 4 heures »].

J'ai vu que tu es chez [current company] maintenant. Ça donne quoi ? Sur quoi tu kiffes en ce moment ?

Faudrait qu'on se cale un vrai échange un de ces quatre.

[Your name]
```

**Reply handling**: easier path. You can suggest a 30-min call directly after one or two exchanges. Peer dynamic = symmetric ask is fine.

## #3 — Ex-direct-report (you managed them)

**Tone**: care-driven, invest in them, not extract from them. This builds the longest-term capital.

### EN

```
Hi [First name],

Your name popped up in my head this week and I realised I had lost track of where you'd ended up. I see you're at [current company] now as [current role]. That's brilliant. Genuinely happy for you.

I still think back to [specific moment, e.g. "the [project] you owned end-to-end" / "how you handled the [tough situation]"]. You were sharper than I gave you credit for at the time.

Would love to hear how the journey's been. No agenda, just curious.

[Your name]
```

### FR

```
Salut [First name],

Ton nom m'est revenu en tête cette semaine et je me suis rendu compte que j'avais perdu ta trace. Je vois que tu es chez [current company] comme [poste actuel]. C'est super, vraiment content pour toi.

Je repense encore parfois à [moment précis, ex. « le [projet] que tu as porté de bout en bout » / « la façon dont tu as géré [situation difficile] »]. Tu étais plus affûté que je ne le réalisais à l'époque.

Curieux de savoir comment le parcours s'est passé. Pas d'agenda, juste pour le plaisir.

[Your name]
```

**Reply handling**: keep investing. Ask about their current challenges, offer a perspective if relevant, do not pitch yourself. The reciprocity comes back later, sometimes years later. Long-game capital.

## #4 — Ex-client (they paid for your work)

**Tone**: respectful, references the engagement outcome, non-transactional. They will assume you want something. The whole job of the message is to prove you don't.

### EN

```
Hi [First name],

Quick note out of the blue. I was [context, e.g. "writing up some thoughts on [domain]" / "talking to someone about [topic from the engagement]"] and your name came up, specifically the [project / engagement] we worked on together. That one stayed with me as one of the cleaner pieces of work I've been part of, largely because of how you ran it from your side.

Saw you're now at [current company]. Hope it's going well. No ask here, just wanted to send a hello and say thanks again.

Best,
[Your name]
```

### FR

```
Bonjour [First name],

Un petit message un peu inattendu. Je [contexte, ex. « rédigeais quelques notes sur [domaine] » / « discutais avec quelqu'un de [sujet de la mission] »] et ton nom est sorti, surtout le [projet / mission] qu'on avait fait ensemble. Cette mission est restée comme l'un des projets les plus propres auxquels j'ai participé, en grande partie grâce à la façon dont tu l'as portée de ton côté.

J'ai vu que tu es chez [current company] maintenant. J'espère que ça se passe bien. Pas de demande, juste envie de te dire bonjour et merci à nouveau.

Bien à toi,
[Your name]
```

**Reply handling**: still no pitch. If they ask what you are up to, say factually (a one-liner, no hard sell). If they ask follow-up questions, answer factually. Let them open the door if they want to. Do not push.

## #5 — Cross-functional / faded (reintroduction)

**Tone**: low-pressure, easy out, no assumption of strong memory. Used for people who knew you but at a distance.

### EN

```
Hi [First name],

Not sure if you remember me. We crossed paths at [company / project / event] back in [year-ish]. I was [your role then] and I think you were [their role then or context].

I've been reconnecting with people from that period and your name was on the list. Saw you're now at [current company]. Interesting move.

If you've got time, would be curious to hear what you've been up to. If not, no worries at all.

Best,
[Your name]
```

### FR

```
Bonjour [First name],

Pas sûr que tu te souviennes de moi. On s'est croisés chez [entreprise / projet / événement] vers [year-ish]. À l'époque j'étais [ton rôle], et toi je crois [son rôle ou contexte].

Je reprends contact avec des gens de cette période et ton nom était sur ma liste. J'ai vu que tu es chez [current company] maintenant. Joli parcours.

Si tu as un moment, je serais curieux de savoir ce que tu fais en ce moment. Sinon pas de souci.

Bien à toi,
[Your name]
```

**Reply handling**: warm up gradually. They may not fully remember you, so reground the relationship in 1-2 messages before suggesting anything. If they do not reply, that is fine. The soft close gave them a graceful exit.

---

## Universal pre-send checklist

Before pasting any DM into LinkedIn:

- [ ] Specific anchor in the opener (not "Hope you're well")
- [ ] Zero mention of jobs / products / asks
- [ ] No CV, no Calendly, no portfolio link
- [ ] Low-pressure close ("would love to hear", not "let's grab a coffee")
- [ ] Word count between 60 and 120
- [ ] Coach has reviewed and approved
- [ ] Tracker updated: Status set to "DM sent" with today's date
